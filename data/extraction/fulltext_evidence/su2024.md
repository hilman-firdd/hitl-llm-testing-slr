# su2024 — Exploratory testing with LLM and knowledge graph (ICSE 2024)
Source read: ACM PDF uploaded by first author, 12 pp., full paper; read 26 Oct 2026.
HUMAN ACTIONS: participants do exploratory testing on Firefox assisted by generated test scenarios and "utilize the top-10 relevant bugs associated with each seed bug during ET" (4.4.2); participants report new bugs, later triaged by Mozilla developers (4.4.3).
TIMING: after generation (use scenarios to explore and judge failures).
EVALUATION: 16 participants (6 PhD, 1 MS, 3 undergraduates, 6 professionals), paired by experience; experimental group uses the approach, control group uses SoapOperaTG; 2 hours; Welch's t-test p = 0.010 (4.4).
OUTCOMES: 36 new bugs (19 confirmed) vs 21 (4.4.3).
LLM: model not named in the extracted text; prompts described with examples. THREATS: "7 THREATS TO VALIDITY".
